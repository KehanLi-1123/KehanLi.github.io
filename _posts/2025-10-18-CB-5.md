## 🧩 1. The Situation

Suppose a bank:

- **Borrows short-term** (e.g., 3-month deposits)  
- **Invests long-term** (e.g., 10-year government bonds)

That creates a **duration mismatch**:

| Position | Duration | DV01 Sign | Exposure |
|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| Deposits (liabilities) | Short (≈0.25y) | Negative | Sensitive to funding-cost rise |
| Government bonds (assets) | Long (≈8–10y) | Positive | Sensitive to bond-yield rise |

Hence, the **assets have higher duration than liabilities** → the balance sheet is **duration long**.  
If yields rise, bond prices fall more than deposit costs adjust — **economic value decreases**.

---

## 🎯 2. ALM Objective

The goal of Asset–Liability Management (ALM) is to **match or hedge** this mismatch so that

$$
\text{Duration}_{\text{assets}} \approx \text{Duration}_{\text{liabilities}}
$$

and thus minimize the sensitivity of **economic value of equity (EVE)** to rate changes:

$$
\text{EVE01} = \frac{\partial(\text{Economic Value of Equity})}{\partial r}
$$

---

## 🧮 3. Quantifying the Mismatch

Let

$$
D_A = \sum_i w_i D_i^{(A)}, \quad D_L = \sum_j w_j D_j^{(L)}
$$

where \(w_i\) is the share of each asset and \(D_i^{(A)}\) its modified duration.

Then define the **duration gap**:

$$
\text{Duration Gap} = D_A - D_L
$$

**Example**

- Assets: 100m in 10-year bonds → \(D_A = 8\)  
- Liabilities: 100m in 3-month deposits → \(D_L = 0.25\)  

So  
$$
\text{Gap} = 7.75 \text{ years}
$$

Approximate EVE sensitivity:

$$
\Delta \text{EVE} \approx -(\text{Duration Gap}) \times \text{EVE} \times \Delta y
$$

If yields rise by \(1\%\):

$$
\Delta \text{EVE} \approx -7.75 \times 100\text{m} \times 1\% = -7.75\text{m}
$$

---

## ⚙️ 4. Duration Matching / Hedging Methods

### (a) **Derivatives: Interest Rate Swaps**

- **Pay-fixed / receive-floating** → reduces duration (hedges long exposure)  
- **Receive-fixed / pay-floating** → increases duration

Example hedge:

> Pay-fixed on a 10-year swap with notional  
> $$100m \times \frac{7.75}{8} = 96.9m$$  
> offsets most of the bond’s duration.

---

### (b) **Change Balance Sheet Composition**

- Shorten assets: sell long bonds, buy short bonds or floating-rate notes.  
- Lengthen liabilities: issue longer-term deposits or debt.  
- Natural hedge: match 5-year loans with 5-year deposits.

---

### (c) **Use Macro ALM Metrics**

| Metric | Focus | Description |
|---------|--------|-------------|
| **Duration Gap** | Value | Long-term sensitivity of EVE |
| **NII01** | Income | Short-term earnings sensitivity |

Banks manage both:  
- Treasury → **EVE01** (economic value risk)  
- Finance → **NII01** (12-month earnings risk)

---

## 📊 5. Example Hedge Design

| Instrument | Position | Notional | Duration | DV01 | Effect |
|-------------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|-----------|
| 10y Gov bond | Long | 100m | 8.0 | +800k | Long duration |
| 3m deposit | Short | 100m | 0.25 | -25k | Short duration |
| 10y pay-fixed swap | Short | 96.9m | 7.9 | -765k | Hedge duration |

Net DV01 ≈ 800k − 25k − 765k ≈ **+10k** → nearly duration-neutral.

---

## 💡 6. Intuition Recap

| Concept | Interpretation |
|----------|----------------|
| **Duration gap > 0** | Exposed to rising rates (EVE ↓) |
| **Duration gap < 0** | Exposed to falling rates (EVE ↓) |
| **Pay-fixed swap** | Reduces duration |
| **Receive-fixed swap** | Increases duration |
| **Balance-sheet adjustments** | Structural hedge via tenor alignment |

---

## 🏦 7. Real Bank Practice

Banks under IRRBB (Interest Rate Risk in Banking Book) typically set:

- **Target duration gap:** near zero  
- **Stress tests:** ±200bp parallel and non-parallel shocks  
- **Reported metrics:**  
  - EVE01 (economic value sensitivity)  
  - NII01 (earnings sensitivity)

Treasury desks use swaps and futures to manage **structural interest-rate risk**, ensuring both **earnings stability** and **capital preservation**.
