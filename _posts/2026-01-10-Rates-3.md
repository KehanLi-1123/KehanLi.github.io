## Correct formulas (use these)

### PV shock (duration + convexity)

$$
\Delta PV \approx -\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\cdot PV \cdot \Delta y \;+\; \frac{1}{2}\,C\cdot PV \cdot (\Delta y)^2
$$

- $$\Delta y$$ is in **decimal** (e.g. 25bp = 0.0025, 50bp = 0.0050)
- $$D_{\mathrm{mod}}$$ in **years**
- $$C$$ in **years^2**

### DV01

$$
DV01 \approx D_{\mathrm{mod}}\cdot PV \cdot 10^{-4}
$$

### Percentage PnL

$$
\%\mathrm{PnL} \approx \frac{\Delta PV}{PV}\times 100\%
$$

Equivalently (in fractional terms):

$$
\frac{\Delta PV}{PV} \approx -\,D_{\mathrm{mod}}\cdot \Delta y \;+\; \frac{1}{2}\,C\cdot (\Delta y)^2
$$
