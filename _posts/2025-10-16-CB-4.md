## 🧭 Overview

**Asset–Liability Management (ALM)** manages a bank’s balance-sheet risks from mismatches between **assets** (loans, securities) and **liabilities** (deposits, borrowings).

Two core goals:

- **Economic Value of Equity (EVE)** — long-term value sensitivity (via **DV01** / **Duration**)
- **Net Interest Income (NII)** — short-term earnings sensitivity (via **NII01**)

---

## 🧩 Example Balance Sheet Mismatch

| Item | Type | Amount | Average Maturity | Rate Type | DV01 (per bp) |
|------|------|---------|------------------|------------|----------------|
| 3-year fixed-rate bond | Asset | £100m | 3 years | Receives fixed | **+£28k** |
| 6-month customer deposit | Liability | £100m | 0.5 year | Pays fixed | **–£3k** |

**Net DV01 = +25k**

→ If rates rise by 1 bp, the bank’s **EVE** falls by £25k.  
The balance sheet is **long duration** — it loses value when rates rise.

---

## 🧾 Step 1 – DV01-Based ALM (EVE Focus)

To neutralize this exposure, ALM uses an **interest rate swap (IRS)**.

| Parameter | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Notional | £90m |
| Swap type | Pay fixed, receive floating |
| Term / Maturity | 3 years (matches bond) |
| Fixed leg | 4.00% semi-annual payments (30/360) |
| Floating leg | 6M SONIA or SOFR (ACT/360) |
| Purpose | Converts part of fixed-rate assets into floating exposure |

---

## 🧮 DV01 Calculation

For a fixed-for-floating swap, the **DV01** (dollar value of one basis point) is:

$$
\text{DV01}_{\text{swap}} = N \times \frac{\displaystyle \sum_{i=1}^{n} P(t_i) \times \Delta_i}{10000}
$$

where:
- \( N \): notional (£90m)  
- \( P(t_i) \): discount factor to the i-th payment  
- \( \Delta_i \): accrual fraction (e.g., 0.5)  
- \( n \): number of fixed coupons

---

### Example (Flat 4% curve)

| i | \(t_i\) (yrs) | \(P(t_i)\) | \(\Delta_i\) | \(P(t_i)\Delta_i\) |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1 | 0.5 | 0.980 | 0.5 | 0.490 |
| 2 | 1.0 | 0.961 | 0.5 | 0.481 |
| 3 | 1.5 | 0.942 | 0.5 | 0.471 |
| 4 | 2.0 | 0.923 | 0.5 | 0.462 |
| 5 | 2.5 | 0.905 | 0.5 | 0.452 |
| 6 | 3.0 | 0.888 | 0.5 | 0.444 |
| **Sum** | | | | **2.80** |

$$
\text{DV01}_{\text{swap}} = 90\,000\,000 \times \frac{2.80}{10000} = £25\,200
$$

Because the bank **pays fixed**, its swap DV01 is **–£25k** (it gains when rates rise).

---

### Net DV01 After Hedging

| Component | DV01 | Sign |
|------------|------|------|
| 3-year fixed bond | +28k | Long rates |
| 6-month deposit | –3k | Short rates |
| Pay-fixed 3y swap | –25k | Short rates |
| **Net** | ≈ 0 | Duration-neutral |

After hedging, the bank’s **EVE** is stable — a textbook **duration/DV01 match**.

---

## ⚙️ Deposit Rate Behavior

| Deposit type | Bank pays | Repricing | Duration | DV01 |
|---------------|------------|------------|-----------|------|
| 6-month term | Fixed | Every 6m | Short | Small negative |
| Overnight | Floating | Daily | ≈ 0 | ≈ 0 |

Thus:
- The **bond (3y fixed)** is long duration  
- The **deposit (6m fixed)** is short duration  
→ The bank is **long rates**, so ALM adds a **pay-fixed swap** to shorten duration.

---

## 🧮 Summary Formula (EVE Focus)

$$
\text{DV01(Assets)} \approx \text{DV01(Liabilities)} + \text{DV01(Hedges)}
$$

## 💰 Step 2 – NII-Based ALM (Earnings Focus)

Now, focus on *earnings* over the **next 12 months**.

| Item | Type | Maturity | Repricing | NII effect (12m) |
|------|------|-----------|------------|------------------|
| 3-year fixed bond | Asset | 3y | No | 0 |
| 6-month fixed deposit | Liability | 0.5y | Yes, at 6m | Refix once |

---

### NII01 (per 1 bp rate move)

Let:
- \( N = 100000000 \)
- \( \Delta r = 0.0001 \)
- Effective period = 0.5 year (6 months)

**Liability NII01 (bank pays):**

$$
\text{NII01}_{\text{deposit}} = -\,N \times \Delta r \times 0.5
$$

$$
= -\,100000000 \times 0.0001 \times 0.5 = -\,£5000
$$

**Asset NII01 (3y fixed):**

$$
\text{NII01}_{\text{bond}} \approx 0
$$

**Total NII01 (unhedged):**

$$
\text{NII01}_{\text{total}} = -£5000
$$

→ A 1 bp rate rise reduces **12-month NII by £5k**.

---

### NII Hedge: Forward-Starting Swap (6x12)

To offset this, ALM can use a **6x12 pay-fixed / receive-floating IRS**.

| Parameter | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Start | in 6 months |
| End | in 12 months |
| Type | Pay fixed, receive floating |
| Notional | chosen to offset £5k/bp |
| Purpose | Receive floating interest in months 7–12 |

**Hedge NII01:**

$$
\text{NII01}_{\text{hedge}} = +\,N_h \times \Delta r \times 0.5
$$

Set:

$$
\text{NII01}_{\text{hedge}} = +5000
\quad \Rightarrow \quad
N_h = \frac{5000}{0.0001 \times 0.5} = 100000000
$$

So a **6x12 pay-fixed / receive-floating** on **£100m notional** neutralizes NII01.

---

## ⚙️ Refinements for Realistic ALM

1. **Deposit beta (β)**  
   If only a fraction of deposit rates move with market rates:

   $$
   \text{NII01}_{\text{deposit}} = -\,\beta N \times \Delta r \times 0.5
   $$

   Then hedge notional: \( N_h = \beta \times 100000000 \).

2. **Deposit floors**  
   If deposits have 0% floors, NII sensitivity is asymmetric (no downside).

3. **Portfolio aggregation**

   $$
   \text{NII01} = \sum_j s_j \beta_j N_j \times
   \frac{\text{days repricing within horizon}}{360} \times 10^{-4}
   $$

   where \( s_j = +1 \) for assets and \( s_j = -1 \) for liabilities.

4. **Dual metrics**  
   Banks manage both:
   - **EVE sensitivity** → long swaps (duration hedge)
   - **NII sensitivity** → short forward swaps (earnings hedge)

## 🧠 Conceptual Comparison

| Aspect | EVE / DV01-based ALM | NII-based ALM |
|--------|----------------------|----------------|
| Horizon | Long-term (EVE) | 12-month earnings |
| Metric | DV01 / Duration | NII01 |
| Objective | Stable market value | Stable income |
| Typical hedge | 3y–10y swaps | FRAs / 6x12 swaps |
| Repricing | Entire curve | Within 12m horizon |

---

## ✅ Key Takeaways

- **DV01-based ALM**: aligns durations of assets and liabilities.  
  $$
  \text{DV01(Assets)} \approx \text{DV01(Liabilities)} + \text{DV01(Hedges)}
  $$

- **NII-based ALM**: aligns repricing within a horizon.  
  $$
  \text{NII01(Assets)} \approx \text{NII01(Liabilities)}
  $$

- **3y fixed asset + 6m deposit** → long duration, short NII.  
  - Hedge DV01: pay-fixed 3y swap  
  - Hedge NII: pay-fixed 6x12 swap

- Real ALM integrates both under **IRRBB (Interest Rate Risk in the Banking Book)**.

## 💡 Final Intuition

- ALM ≠ trading — it’s **internal risk stabilization**.  
- **EVE focus** = value neutrality.  
- **NII focus** = earnings neutrality.  
- Tools: swaps, FRAs, term funding → resilience across rate cycles.
