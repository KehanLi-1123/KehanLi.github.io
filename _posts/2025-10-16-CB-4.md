## 🧭 Overview

**Asset–Liability Management (ALM)** focuses on managing the **interest rate and liquidity mismatches** between a bank’s *assets* (loans, securities) and *liabilities* (deposits, borrowings).

The goal is to stabilize both:

- **Economic Value of Equity (EVE)** → long-term value sensitivity (via **DV01 / Duration**)
- **Net Interest Income (NII)** → short-term earnings sensitivity (via **NII01**)

---

## 🧩 Example Balance Sheet Mismatch

| Item | Type | Amount | Average Maturity | Rate Type | DV01 (per bp) |
|------|------|---------|------------------|------------|----------------|
| 3-year fixed-rate bond | Asset | £100 m | 3 years | Receives **fixed** | **+£28 k** |
| 6-month customer deposit | Liability | £100 m | 0.5 year | Pays **fixed** | **–£3 k** |

**Net DV01 = +25 k** →  
If interest rates rise by 1 bp, the bank’s **EVE falls by £25 k**.  
This means the bank is **long duration** (suffers when rates rise).

---

## 🧾 Step 1 – The Hedge: Pay-Fixed / Receive-Floating Swap

To neutralize this interest-rate exposure, ALM executes an **interest-rate swap (IRS)**.

| Parameter | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| Notional | £90 m |
| Swap type | **Pay fixed, receive floating** |
| Term / Maturity | **3 years** (matches bond maturity) |
| Fixed leg | 4.00 % semi-annual payments (30/360) |
| Floating leg | 6-month SONIA or SOFR (ACT/360) |
| Purpose | Converts part of the fixed-rate asset into floating exposure |

---

## 🧮 Step 2 – DV01 of the Swap

The **DV01** (dollar value of one basis point) measures the change in swap value for a 1 bp parallel shift in the curve:

$$
\text{DV01}_{\text{swap}} = N \times \sum_{i=1}^{n} P(t_i)\,\Delta_i \,/\, 10{,}000
$$

where:
- \( N \): notional (£90 m)
- \( P(t_i) \): discount factor to payment \(t_i\)
- \( \Delta_i \): accrual fraction (0.5 for semi-annual)
- \( n \): number of fixed payments

---

### Example Calculation

Assume flat 4 % discount curve:

| i | \(t_i\) (yrs) | \(P(t_i)\) | \(\Delta_i\) | \(P(t_i)\Delta_i\) |
|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|:-:|
| 1 | 0.5 | 0.980 | 0.5 | 0.490 |
| 2 | 1.0 | 0.961 | 0.5 | 0.481 |
| 3 | 1.5 | 0.942 | 0.5 | 0.471 |
| 4 | 2.0 | 0.923 | 0.5 | 0.462 |
| 5 | 2.5 | 0.905 | 0.5 | 0.452 |
| 6 | 3.0 | 0.888 | 0.5 | 0.444 |
| **Sum** | | | | **2.80** |

$$
\text{DV01}_{\text{swap}} = 90{,}000{,}000 \times 2.80 / 10{,}000 = £25{,}200
$$

Because the bank **pays fixed**, its swap DV01 is **–£25 k** (it gains when rates rise).

---

## ⚖️ Step 3 – Net DV01 After Hedging

| Component | DV01 | Sign |
|------------|------|------|
| 3-year fixed bond | +28 k | Long rates |
| 6-month deposit | –3 k | Short rates |
| **Pay-fixed 3-year swap** | –25 k | Short rates |
| **Net** | ≈ 0 | Duration-neutral |

The bank’s **EVE** is now stable for small rate changes — this is the essence of **duration or DV01 matching** in ALM.

---

## 🏦 Step 4 – Understanding Deposit Rate Behavior

### Fixed-Term Deposit (e.g. 6-month CD)
- Bank **pays a fixed rate** (say 4 % for 6 months)
- Fixed for 0.5 years → short-duration liability  
  → **Small negative DV01**

### Floating / Overnight Deposit
- Bank **pays floating rate** (SONIA / Fed Funds)
- Reprices daily → **Near-zero duration**

### Implication
| Deposit type | Bank pays | Duration | DV01 |
|---------------|------------|-----------|------|
| 6-month fixed | Fixed | Short | – small |
| Overnight | Floating | ≈ 0 | ≈ 0 |

Since the bond asset is long duration (receives fixed 3 y), the balance sheet is **net long rates** →  
ALM **adds a pay-fixed swap** to offset.

## 🧠 Step 5 – Broader ALM Objectives

| Objective | Example ALM Action | Focus Metric |
|------------|-------------------|---------------|
| EVE (value) stabilization | Duration / DV01 matching with swaps | DV01, Duration |
| Earnings stabilization | Hedge NII sensitivity using short swaps / FRAs | NII01 |
| Liquidity resilience | Maintain HQLA buffer, manage LCR/NSFR | Liquidity ratios |
| Structural funding | Adjust mix of fixed vs floating liabilities | Structural gap |

---

## ✅ Summary Formulae

Duration / DV01 neutralization:

$$
\text{DV01(Assets)} \approx \text{DV01(Liabilities)} + \text{DV01(Hedges)}
$$

Earnings-based management:

$$
\text{NII01(Assets)} \approx \text{NII01(Liabilities)}
$$

---

## 💡 Intuitive Summary

- ALM ≠ Trading — it’s **internal risk management** for the bank’s balance sheet.  
- Focus is on **repricing gaps**, **maturity mismatches**, and **interest-rate sensitivity**.  
- Tools include **swaps, futures, term funding**, and **structural balance adjustments**.  
- The goal: smooth **earnings and capital value** across rate cycles.
